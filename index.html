What this is

You've been using Claude Chat. It's smart but it forgets. Every conversation starts at zero. You explain who you are, what Rallee does, your voice rules, what you've decided. Then the window closes and it's gone.

This workspace fixes that. It's a folder on your Mac that Claude Code reads every time you open it — your brand bible, your decisions, your patterns, your past notes. The same Claude you've been using, but tuned to your work and remembering it.

Eleven small skills, three quiet safety hooks, one search tool, and the full Rallee brand bible already token-ised so Claude knows the difference between a refined editorial post and a corporate-stiff one.

The five things you'll use most

Start here. The other four skills are there when you need them.

You type
"Need a square LinkedIn post about [topic]. Brief should hit my brand."
What happens: /design-brief fires. It reads your brand bible (palette, fonts, voice principles), reads your voice rules, returns a Canva-ready brief — exact copy, hex codes, font specs, layout suggestion. Paste into Canva, apply your brand kit, post.
You type
"Critique this: [paste a LinkedIn DM, a candidate email, a client proposal]"
What happens: /critique fires. Scored 1 to 20. Verdict: Ship, Fix, or Kill. Specific edit suggestions for each problem. Harsh on purpose — that's the value.
You type
"Research the boutique recruitment landscape in Sydney for Q2 2026 — what's moving, what's not."
What happens: /research-analyst fires. Multi-source web research with citations, freshness flagged, structured output. No fabricated stats — sources or it didn't happen.
You type
"What does my brand bible say about red usage?"
What happens: /brand-bible fires. Quotes the exact rule from resources/brand/rallee/visual-style.yaml — red as accent only, never background, single moment per asset. Quotes hex codes verbatim.
You type at end of day
"Session close. Capture what we decided today."
What happens: /session-close fires. Asks you for the day's decisions, lessons, blockers. Writes them to memory. Tomorrow when you open the folder, Claude remembers — picks up where you left off.

The rhythm

Three touchpoints a day. Each one is short. The discipline is what compounds.

Morning · 2 min
Orient.
Open the folder in Claude Code. The first message of the session loads SOUL, CLAUDE, USER, LESSONS, MEMORY automatically. Type: "What did I leave open from yesterday?" Claude shows you.
During · as needed
Work the day.
Pull whichever skill the task calls for. LinkedIn post → design brief. Outreach email → write + humanise + critique. New client research → research-analyst. Each is one prompt.
End of day · 3 min
Capture.
Type: "Session close." Claude prompts you for decisions, lessons, anything carrying into tomorrow. Five sentences max. The compounding is in the consistency.

What Claude already knows about you

Once Dave finishes setup with you, this is loaded automatically every session:

  • Your USER.md — who you are, what Rallee does, how you prefer to work, what you don't want from AI
  • Your brand bible — palette (Rallee Red #ea0000 sparingly, Rallee Black, warm white), typography (Futura H1, Inter Display body), voice (refined / intelligent / warm / Australian without colloquial), photography direction (editorial / lifestyle / red wardrobe features)
  • Your lessons — ten hard-won rules about what to never do (e.g., never delete with rm, always visual-verify before saying "done")
  • Your memory — every decision and lesson you capture via /session-close stays in memory/ forever

It does NOT touch your global Claude Code setup. This is a project folder. When you switch to a different folder, this workspace goes quiet.

How updates work

Dave pushes improvements weekly — new skills, better instructions, refinements to existing tools. He'll Telegram you when there's something new worth pulling. When that happens, type:

You type
"Pull latest. Walk me through what changed."
What happens: /update fires. Runs git pull origin main, reads the new entries in CHANGELOG.md, explains each change in plain language. ~30 seconds.

You don't have to pull every change. Wait for Dave to flag the worthwhile ones.

When something feels off

Telegram Dave. Be specific — paste the exact prompt you used and what came back. Every weird response goes into our build log and the next person hits it less.

If you want to start a session over, just close and reopen the folder in Claude Code. No state to corrupt — the workspace is built to be re-openable. Your work is in memory/ and lives separately from any session state.

What this is not

  • Not a replacement for your judgement. Claude drafts. You approve. Especially on anything client-facing.
  • Not a content factory. One on-brand LinkedIn post a week is worth ten generic ones. The skills are for craft, not volume.
  • Not your personal AI. When you open a different folder or visit claude.ai for personal stuff, this workspace doesn't follow you.
  • Not finished. v0.1.1 is the foundation. More skills layer on as you tell Dave what you need.
