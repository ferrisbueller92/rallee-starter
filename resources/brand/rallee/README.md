## TL;DR (for fast-loading)

**Rallee People** — boutique recruiting and team-building agency, Sydney, founded by Bree Hardie. Editorial brand voice. High-touch positioning vs commodity recruiters.

**Locked v1 tokens:**
- Red `#ea0000` (single accent, never background)
- Black `#070707` (primary ink)
- Warm white `#fffcf9` (background)
- Grey `#dddddd` + Light Grey `#f6f2f2` (lines / subtle surfaces)

**Locked typography:**
- H1: Futura, 56/64pt, tracking 0%
- H2: Inter Display Regular, 40/48pt, tracking -2%
- H3: Inter Display Medium, 36/42pt, tracking -2%
- H4: Inter Display Semi Bold, 28/36pt, tracking -1%
- Body: Inter Display Regular, 18/24pt, tracking -1%

**Voice in one line:** refined, intelligent, warm, Australian without colloquial. Curated language. "Rally" verb used sparingly.

**Visual direction in one line:** editorial restraint. Whitespace as premium signal. Red sparingly. Photography lifestyle/editorial, not stock-corporate.

**What Rallee is NOT:** high-volume body shop. Digital recruiter masquerading as boutique. Corporate-stiff. LinkedIn-template-prose.
